Sources & attribution

Every connection in Provenance carries at least one evidence record — a source URL and a paraphrased claim. An edge that cannot cite a source does not exist. These are the corpora the shared knowledge base draws on:

  • MusicBrainz — canonical album identities (release groups), artist credits, personnel and discography relationships. Data licensed CC0 / CC BY-NC-SA.
  • Wikidata — artist influence statements (P737), sitelinks. CC0.
  • Wikipedia(multiple language editions — the album's origin language is read first) — influence claims, paraphrased with attribution. Text under CC BY-SA 4.0; claims paraphrased from Wikipedia inherit attribution and share-alike obligations, which this page and per-claim source links satisfy.
  • Cover Art Archive — album artwork, hotlinked per their usage guidance.
  • Press sources (Rolling Stone, The Guardian, Pitchfork, and others) — linked per claim as evidence; text is never reproduced, only paraphrased with citation.

Claims are extracted by a language model, then independently verified against their cited source before publication; unverifiable citations are flagged, contradicted ones are never published. Machine and human contributions are first-class peers — corrections always require evidence.
